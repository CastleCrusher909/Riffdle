Privacy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Riffdle is a free fan project with no accounts, no ads and no sign-up. It collects as little as possible. Here is all of it.

Visit counts

Riffdle uses GoatCounter to count page visits. It records things like which page was opened, the referring site, browser type and country. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.

Song requests and feedback

If you request a song or send feedback, the text you write is saved so it can be read later. The name or email field is optional and is only used to reply to you. Messages are never sold or shared. To have a message deleted, email the address below.

Saved on your device

Your daily results, streak and multiplayer name are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. This stays on your device and is not sent to Riffdle. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Multiplayer

The name you pick in a multiplayer room is shown to the other players in that room. Rooms exist only while the game is running and are not saved.

Technical information

Like any website, the server sees your IP address when you connect. Riffdle uses it briefly to stop people from spamming the feedback form and does not store it. The hosting provider (Render) may keep standard server logs.

YouTube

After a round, the song's video may be shown using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed. If you play it, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Kids

Riffdle doesn't ask for personal information to play. If you're under 13, please don't put your email in the request or feedback forms.
