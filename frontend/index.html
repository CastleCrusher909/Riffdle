How to play

Riffdle is a free music guessing game. Each round, a song is split into four stems (drums, bass, melody and vocals) and you guess the track as the stems come in one at a time. Play solo or start a live game with friends.

How do you play Riffdle?

Listen to the first stem and type your guess. Each wrong guess or skip adds another stem, so the song gets easier to recognize. Name the title, and the artist for bonus points.

Is Riffdle free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no sign-up.
