About Riffdle

Riffdle is a free music guessing game. Each round, a song is split into four stems — drums, bass, melody, and vocals — and you guess the track as the stems reveal one at a time. Play a quick solo round or start a real-time multiplayer game with friends.

How do you play Riffdle?

Listen to the first stem and type your guess. With each guess, Riffdle layers in another stem so the song gets easier to recognize. Name the title — and the artist for bonus points — before the clip ends.

Is Riffdle free?

Yes. Riffdle runs right in your browser with no sign-up, solo or with friends.
